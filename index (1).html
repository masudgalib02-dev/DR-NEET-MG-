1. Dual Nature of RuBisCO:
RuBisCO has competitive affinity for both CO2 and O2. When temperature is elevated or O2 concentration is high, RuBisCO acts as an oxygenase, forming 1 molecule of 3-PGA (3C) and 1 molecule of Phosphoglycolate (2C).


2. Why It's 25% Carbon Wasteful:
Phosphoglycolate passes through Peroxisome and Mitochondria where 1 CO2 is lost for every 2 molecules of glycolate without synthesizing any ATP or NADPH! Rather, ATP is consumed.


3. C4 Bypass (Kranz Anatomy):
C4 plants use PEP carboxylase (which has ZERO affinity for O2) in mesophyll cells, releasing CO2 right inside Bundle Sheath cells around RuBisCO, ensuring saturated CO2. Hence photorespiration NEVER occurs in C4 plants (e.g., Maize, Sugarcane)!


⚠️ NEET Exam Trap: RuBisCO is absent in mesophyll cells of C4 plants—located ONLY in bundle sheath cells!
